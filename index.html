Our Vision For The Future

To drive productivity, reduce losses, and foster sustainable growth in the poultry industry, ultimately improving nutrition and well-being across rural communities.

We are dedicated to providing accessible and affordable poultry farming solutions, manufacturing high-quality egg incubators, establishing a network of hatching stations, and supplying healthy chicks to empower farmers and combat malnutrition in Rwanda.

Our Core Mission

We empower rural communities by providing affordable, high-quality incubators and fostering a network of hatching stations.

Additionally, we supply healthy one-day-old and thirty-day-old chicks, raised under ideal conditions to enhance productivity, reduce losses, combat malnutrition, and promote sustainable growth in the poultry industry.
